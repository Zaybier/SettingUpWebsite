RPS Game Privacy Policy

Last updated : - 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JOrg Games ,the publishing organization, handles information in connection with Rock-Paper-Scissors Game (hereafter referred to as RPS Game). This policy has been written to be as clear and honest as possible about our setup.

1. Who We Are

The Game is developed and published by JOrg Games. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at: zaybier.JOrg@gmail.com.

2. Our Approach to Data

This game was created with the intention of nothing more than for educational purposes. We do not knowingly collect, store, sell, or share any personal data about you ourselves. We do not operate our own analytics, advertising, or tracking systems. The only data processing connected to the Game comes from the third-party online service we use to provide online connectivity, described below.

3. Epic Online Services (EOS)

The Game uses Epic Online Services ("EOS"), provided by Epic Games, to enable online connection features. When you use these online features, EOS may process certain information in order to authenticate you and establish a connection. This may include:

  • Your Epic account identifier (used to authenticate your session);
  • Technical and connection data (such as device or session information) needed to establish and maintain the online connection.

This processing is carried out by Epic Games as the provider of EOS, under Epic's own privacy policy. We do not receive, store, or have access to your Epic account password, and we do not maintain our own database of player personal data. You can read Epic's privacy policy here:

Epic Online Service Privacy Policy

https://legal.epicgames.com/epicgames/privacy-policy .


Epic Online Service

https://onlineservices.epicgames.com/ .

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed through EOS is used solely for the purpose of providing the Game's online connection functionality — for example, to authenticate your session and connect you to the online service. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties, because we do not collect or store any data ourselves. The only third-party service knowingly involved in this game's online functionality is Epic Online Services (Epic Games), which processes data as described in Section 3 above.

6. Data Retention

Because we do not store personal data ourselves, we have no retention period to apply. Any data processed by EOS is retained by Epic Games in accordance with Epic's own policies and retention practices.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal data, such as the right to access, correct, or delete it. Since we do not hold personal data ourselves, requests relating to data processed by EOS should be directed to Epic Games through the contact options described in Epic's privacy policy.

8. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children below the age required by applicable law to consent to online services in their region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

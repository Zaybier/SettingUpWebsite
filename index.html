About The Game


A quick, simple game of rock-paper-scissors. Make your selection, and let your luck take its course.

Play alone vs AI, or play with another friend*

*Requires an Unreal Engine account

First to win 3 rounds wins the game.

How to play :

Controls :
[1] = Rock
[2] = Paper
[3] = Scissors

Others :
[E] = Interact
[Left Mouse] = Anything else
If both players have the same selection, the round resarts.
